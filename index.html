What you'll be able to do.

You'll be able to understand and apply core macroeconomic models to analyze the behavior of the aggregate economy over time, and measure key economic indicators, including GDP, unemployment, and inflation, using multiple approaches, then interpret what they mean for economic performance and policy.

You'll gain proficiency in analyzing the short-run functioning of the economy with the IS-LM model, the medium-run dynamics of inflation and output with the IS-LM-Phillips Curve (IS-LM-PC) model, and the long-run determinants of growth with the Solow Growth model. You'll also explore the Overlapping Generations (OLG) model, which gives a microeconomic foundation for saving behavior and intertemporal decision-making.

The goal is to build your ability to think analytically about macroeconomic issues, use formal models to explain real-world phenomena, and evaluate the implications of different policy interventions, while strengthening how you communicate economic reasoning through both mathematical and graphical tools.
