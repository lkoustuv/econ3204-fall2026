Steady state, and what moves it

In steady state, output per effective worker \(Y/AN\) is constant. That does not mean living standards stand still. Output per worker is technology times output per effective worker, so it grows at the rate of technological progress:

\( \dfrac{Y}{N} = A \times \dfrac{Y}{AN} \quad\Rightarrow\quad g_{Y/N} = g_A + g_{Y/AN} \) Constant \(Y/AN\) ⇒ \(Y/N\) grows at \(g_A\). On a log scale that is a straight line with slope \(g_A\).
